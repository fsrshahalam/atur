Features

Built for the way the faculty actually schedules exams.

No more chasing spreadsheets over email. ATUR keeps course lists, proposed dates and approvals together, and stops clashes before anyone has to spot them.

Clash detection as you pick a date

When a resource person chooses a date, ATUR checks every program and semester the course is offered in. If the same cohort already has an assessment that day, the date is rejected before it's submitted.

One-click approvals

Approve or reject from a single queue with remarks. Courses shared across programs move together, so one decision updates every linked offering.

Deadline reminders

Resource persons get one email listing every course still missing a date, on the days you choose before the session ends. Admins get a daily summary.

CSV course import

Load a whole session's study plans, courses and resource persons from one template. Re-uploading is safe: submitted dates and approvals are never overwritten.

Calendar views

See the whole faculty's assessment period by month, or just your own courses. The calendar opens on the next upcoming exam, not an empty month.

Exports & public schedule

Download the approved schedule as PDF, Excel or CSV, and share a read-only public link students can search, filter and print.

Secure by default

Sign in with your UiTM Google account. Only registered users get in, every action is checked against your role, and every change is written to the audit log.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Open ATUR while signed in to your UiTM Google account (@uitm.edu.my). There's no separate password. If your browser is signed in to several Google accounts, switch to your UiTM one first.

I can't get in. What should I do?

Only registered users can open the dashboard. Ask your faculty ATUR administrator to add your UiTM email. If you were recently assigned a course, you may already be registered through the course import.

What happens if my chosen date clashes?

ATUR tells you straight away which course already occupies that date for the same program and semester. Pick another date and submit again. Clashing dates are never sent for approval.

Can I change a date after submitting?

Yes. Use Edit / resubmit on My Courses. The new date goes back to the approval queue. If an admin rejects a date, you'll get an email with their remarks and the course moves to the top of your list.

Do students need an account to see the schedule?

No. The public schedule page is read-only and shows only approved dates. It doesn't show resource persons' email addresses.
